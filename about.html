“I started Twisted Loops because the world needed softer things.”

Twisted Loops began as a quiet experiment — a ball of cotton yarn, one hook, and a cup of chai that went cold while the first tote took shape. What started as a hobby to slow down everyday life turned into something bigger: a small studio of hands making bags, pouches, and little pieces of softness for a world moving a little too fast.

Every piece is hand-crocheted in India using naturally dyed cotton and recycled yarns. We make in small batches, often made to order, so nothing we create goes to waste. The imperfect stitches are on purpose — they are the signature of a human hand, not a machine.

We believe fashion should feel like a hug. We believe quality doesn't have to be loud. And we believe the most beautiful things are the ones you can carry, every day, forever.
